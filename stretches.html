All Stretches Reference

A complete list of every stretch in StretchFlow, organized by muscle group.

Neck

Lateral Neck Tilt

How to: Stand or sit tall. Slowly tilt your head toward your right shoulder until you feel a gentle stretch on the left side of your neck. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch sides.

Target: Lateral neck muscles (scalenes, upper trapezius)

Traps

Upper Trap Stretch

How to: Sit up straight. Place your right hand on top of your head and gently pull your head toward your right shoulder while keeping your left shoulder down. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch sides.

Target: Upper trapezius

Shoulders

Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch

How to: Bring your right arm across your chest. Use your left hand to gently press your right arm closer to your body. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch arms.

Target: Posterior deltoid, rotator cuff

Upper Back

Cat-Cow Stretch

How to: Start on your hands and knees. Inhale and arch your back, dropping your belly toward the floor (cow). Exhale and round your spine toward the ceiling, tucking your chin (cat). Repeat for 30–60 seconds.

Target: Thoracic spine, rhomboids, erector spinae

Lower Back

Knee-to-Chest Stretch

How to: Lie on your back. Pull one knee toward your chest, holding it with both hands. Keep your other leg flat on the floor. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch legs.

Target: Lumbar erector spinae, lower back

Chest

Doorway Chest Stretch

How to: Stand in an open doorway. Place your forearms on each side of the door frame at shoulder height. Step one foot forward and lean gently until you feel a stretch across your chest. Hold for 20–30 seconds.

Target: Pectoralis major and minor

Biceps

Wall Bicep Stretch

How to: Stand next to a wall. Place your right palm flat on the wall at shoulder height with your arm straight. Slowly rotate your body away from the wall until you feel a stretch in your bicep. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch arms.

Target: Biceps brachii

Triceps

Overhead Tricep Stretch

How to: Raise your right arm overhead. Bend your elbow so your right hand reaches toward the middle of your upper back. Use your left hand to gently push your right elbow back. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch arms.

Target: Triceps brachii

Forearms

Wrist Flexor Stretch

How to: Extend your right arm in front of you with your palm facing up. Use your left hand to gently pull your fingers downward toward the floor. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch hands.

Target: Wrist flexors, forearm muscles

Glutes

Seated Figure-Four Stretch

How to: Sit on a chair. Cross your right ankle over your left knee. Sit tall and gently lean forward until you feel a stretch in your right glute. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch sides.

Target: Gluteus maximus, piriformis

Hips

Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch

How to: Kneel on your right knee with your left foot flat on the floor in front of you. Push your hips forward gently while keeping your torso upright. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch sides.

Target: Hip flexors (iliopsoas)

Hamstrings

Standing Toe Touch

How to: Stand with your feet hip-width apart. Slowly bend forward at the hips, reaching your hands toward your toes. Keep a slight bend in your knees. Hold for 20–30 seconds.

Target: Hamstrings, lower back

Quads

Standing Quad Stretch

How to: Stand on your left leg. Grab your right ankle behind you and pull your heel toward your glute. Keep your knees together and your torso upright. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch legs.

Target: Quadriceps

Calves

Wall Calf Stretch

How to: Stand facing a wall. Place your hands on the wall. Step your right foot back, keeping it straight with your heel on the ground. Bend your left knee and lean into the wall. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch legs.

Target: Gastrocnemius, soleus

Feet

Plantar Fascia Stretch

How to: Sit down and cross one foot over the opposite knee. Grab your toes and gently pull them back toward your shin until you feel a stretch along the bottom of your foot. Hold for 20–30 seconds, then switch feet.

Target: Plantar fascia, intrinsic foot muscles
